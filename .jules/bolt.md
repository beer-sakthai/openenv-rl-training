## 2026-08-21 - [String Concatenation in Eval Render Loops]
**Learning:** Found a codebase-specific anti-pattern in `eval_hermes_env.py` and `eval_bench_peft.py` where string concatenation (`+=`) was used inside loops to render the `tool_calls` list for every turn of agentic evaluation. Given the recursive and multi-turn nature of evaluation scripts, doing heavy string re-allocation overheads can silently degrade evaluation speed across thousands of turns. A similar anti-pattern existed in `openenv-custom-training/train.py` where `append()` in a loop was used instead of a list comprehension for reward parsing.
**Action:** Always replace `+=` inside rendering loops with a `list` accumulator and `"\n".join(parts)`. Replace loop-based list building with list comprehensions where possible (e.g. `_browsergym_reward`).
